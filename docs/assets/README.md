# Demo assets

- `demo.mp4` (≈ 1.1 MB, 1440×900, 15 fps, H.264 CRF 30 `-tune stillimage`) / `demo.gif` (≈ 2.9 MB, 1200 px, 6 fps, ffmpeg palette with 128 colours, `stats_mode=full`, no dithering, `diff_mode=rectangle`): a 40 s simulated call on the "Haggling rep" scenario (`?scenario=haggling_rep&view=operator`), shown at the top of the root README. It opens in the Debt negotiator view and plays the call with **Watch a call**: first offer 49%, a hold at 49%, a step up to 59%, the rep's lowest number of 65%, a final offer of 62%, and the accept when the rep repeats 65% ($1,300), with the agent's short acknowledgements in the conversation. It then opens the "Took a step up to 59%" row of "How the agent decided" so its explanation can be read, with the final-offer and accept rows above it and the drafted agreement and payment schedule at the top of the right column, and ends on the Creditor rep view of the same call. Recorded 2026-10-10 (Phase 51) from a local server (`LLM_PROFILE=offline`, so no model call) with a headless Chrome screencast at 1440×900 in the light theme, as lossless PNG frames (a first take from JPEG frames through the MP4 gave a 4.8 MB GIF at 1200 px / 5 fps, against 2.7 MB from PNG frames: compression noise defeats frame differencing). The autoplay pause was raised from the console's 1.2 s to 2.6 s per step (`autoplay_pause_ms` in the start message) so each step can be read; the call itself is unchanged. The frames were resampled to a fixed 30 fps sequence by their screencast timestamps, then encoded twice from that sequence (the GIF straight from the PNG frames, not from the MP4). README media total (GIF + MP4) ≈ 3.97 MB of the 5 MB cap in `tests/unit/test_docs.py`.
- `console-operator-autoplay.jpg` (1440×900): the Debt negotiator view after the same simulated call, with the 59% step row open and the drafted agreement at the top of the right column (Phase 51).
- `console-creditor-autoplay.jpg` (1440×900): the Creditor rep view (`?view=rep`) after the same simulated call (Phase 51).
