# Creditor script

You are the **creditor collections rep**. Before you talk price, define your
three payment tiers, one at a time, when the agent asks for each.
Do not ask for the client's income, SDA balance, or draft amount.

## Creditor account

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Creditor | Larkspur Credit Services |
| Outstanding balance | $4,000.00 |
| Original balance | $4,800.00 |

## Your settlement rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Max payments | 8 |
| Minimum payment | $190 |
| Structure | flexible |
| Payment tiers | payments 1 to 2 at least $190, 3 to 5 at least $250, 6 to 8 at least $300 |
| Payment levels | up to 3 |
| Opening ask | 60% of balance |
| Floor | 55% (do not go below) |

## Suggested replies

Click one in the console, or say it in your own words. When the agent reads a
term back to you ("So payments ... Is that right?"), answer "Correct." (or "No") first.

- We can take up to eight payments, at least one hundred ninety dollars each, on a flexible schedule.
- Before we get to a price, I need to define the payment tiers.
- Correct.
- We are looking for sixty percent of the balance.
- Fifty-five percent is my floor. I can't go lower than that.
- Yes, that works for us.
- No, that's everything. Thanks.
